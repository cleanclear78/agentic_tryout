Mandate mismatch

Model structure does not fully reflect sector, regional, thematic, and client-specific universes. A one-size-fits-all approach limits use of richer datasets (e.g. GICS granularity).

Stable vs transient risk

Persistent factors support reporting, but fast-moving themes (AI, macro, crowding) are not systematically captured.

Risk vs portfolio construction

Designed for risk measurement, not explicitly aligned with optimizer behavior or quant alpha integration.

Expert bottleneck

Infrastructure has improved, but high-value customization and experimentation still depend on specialized modeling expertise.

With infrastructure in place, the opportunity is to evolve toward a more flexible, practitioner-aligned modeling approach.
